ABOUT MY WORK

E-Commerce Catalog Freelancer

I provide freelance catalog and listing support for e-commerce sellers and agencies. My practical work includes Myntra Seller Panel operations, CSV/Excel filling, SKU and size mapping, product attributes, image uploads and listing corrections.

I can follow client-provided templates and instructions, keep catalog information organized and communicate issues that require seller or marketplace support.

I am available for project-based, per-product, bulk and ongoing remote catalog work. The scope and price are confirmed before starting.
